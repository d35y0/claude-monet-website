Title: Water Lilies
Painter: Claude Monet
Dimensions: 35 3/8 x 37 1/16 in
Year Produced: 1906
Physical Description: Oil on canvas
Location: The Art Institute of Chicago, USA

Claude Monet's "Water Lilies" (1906), is a part of the series Water Lilies that is composed of over 250 water lilies paintings. This painting is a part of his third series of these works. In this canvas, which presents a spatial ambiguity, the artist shifted his perspective downward, concentrating solely on the pond's surface. Here, a cluster of vegetation floats amidst reflections of the sky and trees.
