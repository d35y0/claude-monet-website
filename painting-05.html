Title: Woman with a Parasol
Painter: Claude Monet
Dimensions: 39 x 32 in
Year Produced: 1875
Physical Description: Oil on canvas
Location: National Gallery of Art, Washington

Claude Monet's “Woman with a Parasol” (1875), features his wife, Camille Monet and their son Jean Monet in a meadow near Argenteuil. Through his models’ placement, Monet implies that his wife and son paused during their stroll for him to paint their likenesses. The brevity of the portrayed moment is conveyed through lively brushstrokes and vibrant colors, defining features of the style Monet helped shape.
