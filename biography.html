Oscard-Claude Monet was born in November 14, 1840 and raised in the town of Le Havre in France’s Normandy region. Monet was a French artist and pioneer to the impressionist art movement, hence why he was often referred to as the father of Impressionism. Monet's passion for art blossomed in his early years. He dedicated countless hours to filling his books with sketches and caricatures of his peers, laying the foundation for his future acclaim in the art world.

The term "Impressionism" originated from Monet's painting titled Impression, Sunrise, which debuted in the first Impressionist exhibition organised by the Société Anonyme des Artistes on the Boulevard des Capucines in Paris. As the Impressionist movement gained recognition and acceptance, Monet's role as a pioneering figure became increasingly apparent. His dedication to this innovative style played a pivotal role in establishing Impressionism as an influential art movement.
