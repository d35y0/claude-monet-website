Title: The Luncheon
Painter: Claude Monet
Dimensions: 231.5 x 151.5 cm
Year Produced: 1868
Physical Description: Oil on canvas
Location: Städel Museum, Germany

Claude Monet's "The Luncheon" (1868) depicts a scene of leisure and social interaction. In this work, Monet captures his family, a guest and a maidservant during luncheon. The painting depicts everyday activities within the Monet household, with the setting placed in Étretat.
